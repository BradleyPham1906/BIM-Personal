documentation page, the licence file, the npm registry record and tarball, or the app's code), not in

or run. Downloads were read in a scratch folder outside the repository.

## Corrections

1. **Fact 4 (the conflict between the two notes).** The 2019 paper was read in full; it is level P. Its
   Table 2 gives, over five transfers of 8, 7, 7, 14 and 48 edits: exact locators 0, 0, 0, 100, 100
   percent; the 2008 semantic locators (best case by hand) 0, 100, 28.6, 100, 75 percent; the paper's
   own meta-locators 100, 100, 100, 100, 93.8 percent. The note that says "semantic keys kept 93.8 to
   100 percent" attributes the meta-locators' result to semantic keys; the note that says only the
   abstract was reachable is out of date. The test is a transfer of edits from a source model to a
   changed target, checked by hand, on five cases.
2. **Fact 1.** The 2006 paper could not be read from here (hosts named in the table). The split
   formula and the ceil in Repeat stay at U until it is read. Fact 2 shows that CityEngine's own
   reference does not state a rounding rule, so it cannot stand in for the paper.
3. **Fact 7.** Dirty bits give minimality, not early cutoff; early cutoff needs verifying (or
   constructive) traces. Excel's dirty bits go with a restarting scheduler, Make's with a topological one.
4. **Fact 9.** Autodesk's help page lists Optimize, Randomize, Space evenly, Like this, and names no
   algorithm. "Cross Product" and "NSGA-II" (customised; crossover 0.8, mutation 0.4) come from the
   Generative Design Primer at generativedesign.org, whose pages do not name their publisher. The
   "population at least three times the inputs" figure was not found in the page read.
5. **Fact 12.** "Deterministic" is an inference from the pseudocode, not the paper's word. The paper
   adds that the input order matters, that optimality is not guaranteed, and that sibling order is lost.
6. **Fact 13c.** UNDO_MAX is 50, and the snapshot is a fixed list of twenty fields, not the whole
   state: a new top-level field must be added to both bimSnapshotState and bimRestoreState.
7. **Fact 13g.** True for bim/ and core/, but analysis drawings already carry `dg` (a, k, gen, newer,
   orphan) and linked clones carry `linkSourceId`; these are the app's existing "made by" fields.
8. **Fact 14.** The header values are on MDN's crossOriginIsolated page, not the SharedArrayBuffer
   page. MDN does not name file://; it says register() rejects any scheme other than http: or https:.
9. **Fact 5 (caution, not a correction).** wasmBinary is honoured by manifold.js but is absent from
   manifold.d.ts, which types only locateFile.
10. **Fact 13a (detail).** The order used for a cycle's nodes is the dirty set's key order; "model
    order" is the message's wording. A throwing visitor is also skipped with ok:true.
