Phone XR lab

Measures what your iPhone can do for cardboard VR with hands and physics. Run the steps in order, in Safari and again from the Home Screen icon (Share, Add to Home Screen). Then tap Copy results and paste them to Claude.

6. Hand tracking (MediaPipe 1.2.3)

    
Needs the camera from step 3. Hold one hand 40 to 60 cm in front of the back camera for 20 s per run.

    

      

    


  
7. Your cardboard viewer

    
Defaults are Google Cardboard v2. If your viewer has a QR code, scan it with the Camera app and paste the address
      it opens here; Claude decodes it later. Or measure with a ruler.

    
    

      
      
      
      
      
      
    


  
8. Into the headset

    
A small table of blocks in front of you. Turn your head to look around. With camera and hands on, push the blocks
      with your hand, or pinch near one to pick it up. Tap the screen edge (or Exit) to leave. The run logs the frame rate
      every minute; a 10 minute run shows whether the phone slows down as it heats.
