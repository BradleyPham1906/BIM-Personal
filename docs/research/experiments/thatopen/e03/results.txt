What it shows. 12 of 13 agree, over http and from file:// alike. The 13th, FM_ARC_DigitalHub, is the one where cc-ifc
stops at the import's 1,500,000-triangle cap (its curved B-reps sampled 20 times finer than needed, V196 section 4) and
drops 109 elements; That Open reads all 705, which is the count V196 measured for both engines without the cap. So That
Open matches V196's reference on all 13. Its triangles are within 3% of cc-ifc's except where cc-ifc over-samples.
On the way (fixed before this run, each with a unit test): class names (IFCBUILDINGELEMENTPROXY was spelled
IfcBuildingelementproxy: web-ifc's GetNameFromTypeCode is empty before Init, so the names now come from web-ifc's schema
namespaces); spaces (That Open draws IfcSpace, V196 never imported them as solids: skipped); IFC4.3 facility parts and
spatial zones (not in That Open's element list: added through IfcLoader's instanceCallback); ids over 999,999
(formatted to 6 figures they collided: schependomlaan lost 195 coverings' data; ids are now never formatted).

12.8 s against 2.5 s; advanced_model 10.4 s against 4.0 s); the time is its IFC-to-fragments conversion (dental_clinic:
convert 3.4 s, data 0.7 s, geometry 0.5 s). FM_ARC_DigitalHub is the exception: 2.8 s against 11.8 s.
