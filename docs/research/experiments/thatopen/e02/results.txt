What each run does: a That Open world (SimpleScene, SimpleRenderer, OrthoPerspectiveCamera) in an 800x600 div;
FragmentsManager on the inline worker (http: module worker; file://: toClassicWorker + classicWorker); IfcLoader with
autoSetWasm false and the wasm handed over by a fetch wrapper from BIMWEBIFC_WASM; load, frame, draw; then the data
the import needs (getCategories, getSpatialStructure, getItemsWithGeometry, getGuidsByLocalIds, getItemsData with
IsDefinedBy and ContainedInStructure, getItemsGeometry), a ray at the view's centre (Raycasters), a clipping plane
(Clipper.createFromNormalAndCoplanarPoint), LengthMeasurement made, getItemsVolume of the first item.
