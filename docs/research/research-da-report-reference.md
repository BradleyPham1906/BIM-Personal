# V178 reference: a Stage 1 DA report generated from the model

The owner's reference for V178 (Presentation P3), from four screenshots in the owner's Notion (2026-11-13). The sheets
in them say "Areas indicative, from the Giraffe model": the product shown is Giraffe's report tool (the owner filed it
as Snaptrude). Described here in our own words; the screenshots are not committed (the repository is public). Level U:
seen in pictures, not tried. V178's research checks each against the app and the practice of DA (development
application) reports, and asks the owner's decisions.

## 1. Layout

- R1 The report sits in a panel beside the model and map (model left, report right); each panel's menu: split
  vertical, split horizontal, collapse, fullscreen, close, tab position, tab style.
- R2 The model keeps its map underlay, greyed, while the report is open.

## 2. The report panel

- R3 Four figure cards at the top: buildings, GFA (m2), dwellings, site coverage (%).
- R4 Generate report, then Regenerate report; the count of sheets and the paper size ("6 sheets, A3").
- R5 Project fields: project, address, client, prepared by, date, revision, job number.
- R6 Look: silhouette width (px), crease lines (px), card texture (m), ambient light, ambient occlusion on/off and its
  strength (%), sun shadows on/off, diagram entities on/off, the angle the axonometric is viewed from (35 degrees).
- R7 Context and solar: context plan scale (1:1000), map underlay on/off, shadow study day (21 June), UTC offset,
  three study times (9:00, 12:00, 15:00).
- R8 Prevailing winds: a list, each a label and the direction it comes from (for example summer north-east breezes
  45, southerly changes 180, winter westerlies 270); add and remove.

## 3. The sheets (A3 landscape, one title block)

- R9 Title block on every sheet: project, address, client, prepared by, date, scale, drawing number (DA-01...),
  revision, sheet n of N, and a "concept only, areas indicative" note; a sheet title and subtitle at the top right
  with a short paragraph of what the sheet shows.
- R10 DA-01 Context plan: the map greyed, the site boundary as a red dash-dot line, the sun's path on the solstices
  and the equinox drawn round the site, the prevailing winds as arrows at the edge, north arrow, scale bar, a legend,
  and the site's figures (area, latitude and longitude, sun times).
- R11 DA-02 Site coverage: building footprints hatched, landscape and hardscape areas, a coverage table with a
  stacked bar (building footprint, deep soil, landscape, hardscape, unallocated) and site area, GFA and floor space
  ratio.
- R12 DA-03 Massing diagrams: the proposal as a card model (axonometric) and three small shadow studies at the study
  times; a legend of the diagram elements.
- R13 DA-04 Building axonometric: one colour per building, a leader to each with its name, ID, GFA, NSA, dwellings
  and storeys; a building schedule (storeys, height, GFA, NSA, dwellings) with totals.
- R14 DA-05 Sections and elevations, with a key plan marking where each is cut.
- R15 DA-06 Key views: perspectives from the street, with a key plan marking each camera.
- R16 A sheet opens full-screen, with previous and next.

## 4. The model's look while designing

- R17 Card model: each building as stacked floor plates with a line at each floor; landscape shapes in greens; trees
  as soft discs; movement as dashed arrows (diagram entities).
